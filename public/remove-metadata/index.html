Why this matters

Photos store EXIF metadata: the GPS coordinates where the picture was taken, the device that took it, and the timestamp. Post or send that photo and you may be handing over your home address without realizing it. This tool reads that metadata in your browser so you can see it, then rebuilds the image without it — losslessly for JPEG and PNG, so the picture itself is untouched.

Everything runs on your device. The image is never uploaded, and you can prove it — turn off your Wi-Fi and it still works.
