What this removes

Every photo carries hidden metadata: where it was taken, the device, the timestamp. This reads it in your browser, then rebuilds the file without it. JPEG and PNG stay pixel-for-pixel identical; other formats are re-encoded. The download keeps only the image’s orientation, and nothing is uploaded.
