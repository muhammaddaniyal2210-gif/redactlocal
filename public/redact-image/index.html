Real redaction, not a sticker

Covering something with a shape in most editors leaves the original pixels underneath — they can resurface when the file is converted or inspected. This tool rewrites the pixels: a black box replaces them with solid black, and pixelate destroys the detail by averaging it into blocks. The exported image is re-encoded from scratch, so the original data — and the file's EXIF/GPS metadata — is gone.

All of it happens on your device. The image is never uploaded; turn off your Wi-Fi and it still works.
