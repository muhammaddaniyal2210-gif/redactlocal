How it works

  1. Open your PDFEvery page appears as a thumbnail you can rearrange.
  2. RearrangeDrag to reorder (or use move buttons), rotate, and delete pages.
  3. ExportDownload a new PDF with your changes, text intact.

Why organize with RedactLocal?

Fixing a sideways scan or dropping a blank page shouldn’t require uploading the whole document. RedactLocal rearranges pages on your device and copies them without rasterizing, so your text stays selectable, quality is preserved, and the file never leaves your machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Pages are reorganized locally in your browser and the file is never sent to RedactLocal.

Will quality or text be affected?

No. Pages are copied with text and vectors intact; only their order and rotation change. Nothing is rasterized.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Drag pages on desktop, or use the move buttons on each page — these work reliably on touch screens where drag-and-drop can be unreliable.
