How it works

  1. Open your PDFDrop it in to see a thumbnail of every page.
  2. Choose a splitSelect pages, type ranges like 1-3 / 4-8, or split every page apart.
  3. DownloadGet one PDF, or a ZIP when the split produces several files.

Why split with RedactLocal?

Sharing one section of a document shouldn’t mean uploading the whole thing to a stranger’s server. RedactLocal splits the PDF on your device and copies pages directly, so the parts you keep stay text-accurate and the original never leaves your machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It’s read and split locally in your browser, and never sent to RedactLocal.

What page selections are supported?

Click thumbnails to select pages, or type a list and ranges like 1,3,5-8. You can also split by several ranges at once, or separate every page into its own file.

Will the split pages lose quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly, so text stays selectable and nothing is rasterized or re-compressed.
