Why use RedactLocal?

Photos and scans often hold more than you intend to share — and uploading them to a conversion site hands that data to a third party. RedactLocal assembles the PDF on your own device, so the images never leave it. It’s quick, works offline, and keeps your originals untouched.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is built locally in your browser from the images you add. Nothing is sent to RedactLocal.

Which formats can I use?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP. You can combine different formats in one PDF and drag them into any order first.

What happens to transparent PNGs?

A PDF page has no transparency, so transparent areas appear over the white page — effectively a clean white background. The rest of the image is unchanged.

Will the PDF be huge?

No. JPGs are embedded as-is, and very large images are scaled to a sensible resolution, so the PDF stays a reasonable size without visible quality loss at normal viewing.
