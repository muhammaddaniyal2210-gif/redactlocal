About & Contact

Protect sensitive information. Keep control of your files.

RedactLocal brings PDF and image redaction, PDF text inspection, and photo metadata removal into one browser-based toolkit. Your files are processed on your device, without being uploaded to a server.

Our mission

Sharing a document or image should not mean sharing everything it contains. Our mission is to make it straightforward to remove sensitive content, identify recoverable PDF text, and clear personal metadata before files are shared.

We build tools that keep file processing on your device and make privacy part of everyday work. By bringing these capabilities together in your browser, RedactLocal gives individuals and teams a practical way to prepare files for sharing while retaining control of the originals.
